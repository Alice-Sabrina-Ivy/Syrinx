# Speech-scoped pitch search range (60–400 Hz) — 2026-06-10

User decision following the "how do other tools handle this" discussion:
no calibration step (zero-interaction design holds), but constrain the
detector's search range to 60–400 Hz instead of the Praat-generic
50–600 Hz. Rationale: the UI's display ceiling is 400 Hz
(PITCH_DISPLAY_RANGE), so detections above it were never displayable —
but they WERE the error surface for 3–4× harmonic locks: 218 of the
2026-05-26 session's two-octave spike runs sat at 450–600 Hz, and the
screenshot spikes plateaued at the 400 Hz display clip.

Speech corpora improve or hold — FDA octave errors halve. Session
improves on every metric; the flip rate drops below Praat's own
self-flip rate on this material (4.27 %). Sole cost: vocadito −1.6 pp —
singing content above 400 Hz can no longer be represented (some >400
notes alias octave-down rather than nulling; the display jump-break
detaches them). Accepted deliberately: Syrinx is a speech-training
tool whose UI caps at 400 Hz; sustained singing above 400 Hz is out of
scope. Revisit the ceiling alongside the display range if the scope
ever changes.

Harness note: stage R initially mis-scored both configs (corpus correct
~49 %) because the sweep's decode-delay realignment defaulted to
lookback 4 while the tracker now defaults to 2 — fixed by passing
lookback explicitly. Worth remembering: the realignment fallback in
ac-tuning-sweep.js must match the tracker config.
