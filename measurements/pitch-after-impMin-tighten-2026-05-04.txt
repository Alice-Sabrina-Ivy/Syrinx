Key observations:

  1. The Pareto-prediction held quantitatively: real-speech female mean
     -1.2 Hz, male unchanged, sub-locks -6. This was exactly what the
     sweep at cell (relK2=0.50, impMin=0.010, absOk=0.15) reported.

  2. The accuracy-test female F0 mean dropped much more than the
     real-speech full-corpus number (-4.4 vs -1.2). That's because the
     accuracy-test subset (5 per vowel x 12 vowels = 60 women) includes
     proportionally more vowels with halving-prone characteristics, so
     it benefits more from the same change. The full-corpus number is
     the more statistically robust signal.

  3. Female F0 still FAILS the < 10 Hz target in accuracy-test. That
     was anticipated by the sweep analysis -- the Pareto frontier maxes
     out at ~14 Hz on the full corpus, ~15 on this subset. Closing the
     gap requires an algorithmic upgrade (pYIN / MPM / SWIPE'),
     not further constant tuning. See
     measurements/harmonic-gate-analysis-2026-05-04.md.

  4. All synthetic stress tests (2nd harmonic, 3rd harmonic, octave-
     doubling, octave-tripling) pass without a single regression --
     consistent with the sweep showing 5/5 + 4/4 across all 84 cells.

  5. No regression in any other test suite. Smoothing-test still 32/32,
     comprehensive still 88/88, yin-harmonic still 12/12.
